## Competitor read (two UGC videos from "sleep sounds / white noise app / sleep app" searches)
- Both are human on-camera videos, not slideshows. Pattern: relatable night-time pain, the app named around the middle, the library shown with pretty covers, "is it free?" answered.
- Our slideshow copies the mechanism, not the content: pain (slide 1) -> app named (slide 2) -> real covers (3-5) -> fade-out (6) -> "free to start" + search line (7). No fake story, no fake app screen.
